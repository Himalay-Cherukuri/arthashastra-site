Chapter XI: Death with or Without Torture

B04.C11 · Book IV: The Removal of Thorns · pp. 324–327

Death with or Without Torture

P001 WHEN a man murders another in a quarrel, he shall be tortured to death. When a person wounded in a fight dies within seven nights, he who caused the wound shall be put to instantaneous death (suddhavadhah). If the wounded man dies within a fortnight, the offender shall be punished with the highest amercement. If the wounded man dies within a month, the offender shall be compelled to pay not only a fine of 500 panas, but also an adequate compensation (to the bereaved). p.324 500 panas fine 500 panas 500 seven amercement compensation fine highest-amercement punishment torture

P002 When a man hurts another with a weapon, he shall pay the highest amercement; when he does so under intoxication, his hand shall be cut off; and when he causes instantaneous death, be shall be put to death. p.324 amercement death highest-amercement mutilation

P003 When a person causes abortion in pregnancy by striking, or with medicine, or by annoyance, the highest, middlemost, and first amercements shall be imposed respectively. p.325 amercement

P004 Those who cause violent death either to men or women, or those who are in the habit of often going to meet prostitutes (abhisáraka), those who inflict unjust punishment upon others, those who spread false or contemptuous rumours, who assault or obstruct travellers on their way, who commit house-breaking, or who steal or cause hurt to royal elephants, horses, or carriages shall be hanged. p.325 prostitute punishment abhisáraka

P005 Whoever burns or carries away the corpses of the above offenders shall meet with similar punishment or pay the highest amercement. p.325 amercement highest-amercement punishment

P006 When a person supplies murderers or thieves with food, dress, any requisites, fire, information, any plan, or assistance in any way, he shall be punished with the highest amercement. When he does so under ignorance, he shall be censured. p.325 amercement highest-amercement punishment

P007 Sons or wives of murderers or of thieves shall, if they are found not in concert, be acquitted; but they shall be seized if found to have been in concert. p.325

P008 Any person who aims at the kingdom, who forces entrance into the king's harem, who instigates wild tribes or enemies (against the king), or who creates disaffection in forts, country parts, or in the army shall be burnt alive from head to foot. p.325 enemy king burning

P009 If a Bráhman does similar acts, he shall be drowned. p.325 brahman drowning bráhman

P010 Any person who murders his father, mother, son, brother, teacher, or an ascetic, shall be put to death by burning both his head and skin; if he insults any of the above persons, his tongue shall be cut off; if he bites any limb of these persons, be shall be deprived of the corresponding limb. p.325 ascetic death mutilation

P011 When a man wantonly murders another, or steals a herd of cattle, he shall be beheaded. p.326

P012 A herd of cattle shall be considered to consist of not more than ten heads. p.326 ten

P013 When a person breaks the dam of a tank full of water, he shall be drowned in the very tank; of a tank without water, he shall be punished with the highest amercement; and of a tank which is in ruins owing to neglect, he shall be punished with the middle-most amercement. p.326 amercement drowning highest-amercement middlemost-amercement punishment

P014 Any man who poisons another and any woman who murders a man shall be drowned. p.326 drowning

P015 Any woman who murders her husband, preceptor, or offspring, sets fire to another's property, poisons a man or cuts off any of the bodily joints of another shall be torn off by bulls, no matter whether or not she is big with a child, or has not passed a month after giving birth to a child. p.326

P016 Any person who sets fire to pasture lands, fields, yards prepared for threshing out grains, houses, forests, of timber or of elephants shall be thrown into fire. p.326 yards

P017 Any person who insults the king, betrays the king's council, makes evil attempts (against the king), or disregards the sanctity of the kitchens of Bráhmans shall have his tongue cut off. p.326 brahman king mutilation bráhmans

P018 When a man other than a soldier steals weapons or armour, he shall be shot down by arrows; if he is a soldier, he shall pay the highest amercement. p.326 soldier amercement highest-amercement

P019 He who castrates a man shall have his generative organ cut off. p.327 mutilation

P020 He who hurts the tongue or nose of another shall have his fingers cut off. p.327 mutilation fingers

P021 * Such painful punishments (klesadanda) as the above have been laid down in the Śástras of great sages; but it has been declared as just to put to simple death those offenders who have not been cruel. p.327 śástras

P022 [Thus ends Chapter XI, "Death with or without torture" in Book IV, "The Removal of Thorns” of the Arthasástra of Kautilya. End of the eighty-eighth chapter from the beginning.] p.327
