Chapter XII: Sexual Intercourse with Immature Girls

B04.C12 · Book IV: The Removal of Thorns · pp. 327–331

Sexual Intercourse with Immature Girls

P001 HE who defiles a maiden of equal caste before she has reached her maturity shall have his hand cut off or pay a fine of 400 panas; if the maiden dies in consequence, the offender shall be put to death. p.327 400 panas fine 400 panas 400 death fine mutilation

P002 He who defiles a maiden who has attained maturity shall have his middle finger cut off or pay a fine of 200 panas, besides giving an adequate compensation to her father. p.327 200 panas fine 200 panas 200 compensation fine mutilation

P003 No man shall have sexual intercourse with any woman against her will. p.327

P004 He who defiles a willing maiden shall pay a fine of 54 panas, while the maiden herself shall pay a fine of half the amount. p.328 54 panas fine 54 panas 54 half fine

P005 When a man impersonates another man who has already paid the nuptial fee to a woman (parasulkopadháyám), he shall have his hand cut off or pay a fine of 400 panas, besides making good the nuptial fee. p.328 400 panas fine 400 panas 400 fine mutilation parasulkopadháyám

P006 No man who has connection with a maiden that has passed seven menses and has not yet succeeded in marrying her, though she has been betrothed to him, shall either be guilty or pay any compensation to her father; for her father has lost his authority over her in consequence of having deprived her so long of the result of her menses. p.328 seven compensation

P007 It is no offence for a man of equal caste and rank to have connection with a maiden who has been unmarried three years after her first menses. Nor is it an offence for a man, even of different caste, to have connection with a maiden who has spent more than three years after her first menses and has no jewellery on her person; for taking possession of paternal property (under such circumstances) shall be regarded as theft. p.328 three

P008 Any person who, while pretending to secure a bride to a particular person, ultimately obtains her for a third person shall be fined 200 panas. p.328 200 panas fine 200 panas 200 fine

P009 No man shall have sexual intercourse with any woman against her will. p.328

P010 If a person substitutes in marriage another maiden for the one he has before shown, he shall, if the substitute is of the same rank, be fined 100 panas, and 200 panas if she is of lower rank. The substituted maiden shall be fined 54 panas, while the offender shall also be compelled to return both the nuptial fee and the amount of expenditure (incurred by the bridegroom). p.328 100 panas 200 panas 54 panas fine 100 panas fine 54 panas 100 200 54 one fine

P011 When a man refuses to live in marriage a particular maiden as agreed upon, he shall pay double the above fine. p.329 double fine

P012 When a man substitutes in marriage a maiden of different blood or is found to have bestowed false praises (on her quality), he shall not only pay a fine of 200 panas and return the nuptial fee, but also make good the expenditure. p.329 200 panas fine 200 panas 200 fine

P013 No man shall have sexual intercourse with any woman against her will. p.329

P014 When a woman being desirous of intercourse, yields herself to a man of the same caste and rank, she shall be fined 12 panas, while any other woman who is an abettor in the case shall be fined twice as much. Any woman who abets a man in having intercourse with a maiden against her will shall not only pay a fine of 100 panas, but also please the maiden providing her with an adequate nuptial fee. p.329 100 panas 12 panas fine 100 panas fine 12 panas 100 12 twice fine

P015 A woman who, of her own accord, yields herself to a man shall be a slave to the king. p.329 king slave

P016 For committing intercourse with a woman outside a village, or for spreading false report regarding such things, double the usual fines shall be imposed. p.329 double fine

P017 He who carries off a maiden by force shall be fined 200 panas; if the maiden thus carried off has golden ornaments on her person, the highest amercement shall be imposed. If a number of persons abduct a maiden, each of them shall be punished as above. p.329 200 panas fine 200 panas 200 amercement fine highest-amercement punishment

P018 When a man has connection with a harlot's daughter, he shall not only pay a fine of 54 panas, but also give her mother sixteen times her daily income. p.330 54 panas fine 54 panas 54 sixteen fine

P019 When a man defiles the daughter of his own male or female slave, he shall not only pay a fine of 24 panas, but also provide the maiden with an adequate nuptial fee (sulka) and jewellery (ábaddhya). p.330 24 panas fine 24 panas 24 slave fine ábaddhya

P020 When a man has connection with a woman who has been held in slavery on account of certain ransom due from her, he shall not only pay a fine of 12 panas, but also provide the woman with dress and maintenance. p.330 12 panas fine 12 panas 12 fine

P021 Abettors in all the above cases shall each have the same punishment as the principal offender. p.330 punishment

P022 A relative, or a servant of an absentee husband may take the latter's wife of loose character under his own protection (samgrihníyat = may marry her). Being under such protection, she shall wait for the return of her husband. If her husband, on his return, entertains no objection, both the protector and the woman shall be acquitted. If he raises any objection, the woman shall have her ears and nose cut off, while her keeper shall be put to death as an adulterer. p.330 death mutilation samgrihníyat

P023 When a man falsely accuses another of having committed theft while in reality the latter is guilty of adultery, the complainant shall be fined 500 panas. p.330 500 panas fine 500 panas 500 fine

P024 He who lets off an adulterer by receiving gold shall pay a fine of eight times the value of the gold (he received). p.330 eight fine

P025 (Adultery may be proved by circumstances such as) hand to hand fight, abduction, any marks made on the body of the culprits, opinion of experts on consideration of the circumstances, or the statements of women involved in it. p.330

P026 When a man rescues a woman from enemies, forests, or floods, or saves the life of a woman who has been abandoned in forests, forsaken in famine, or thrown out as if dead, he may enjoy her as agreed upon during the rescue. p.331 enemy

P027 A woman of high caste, with children and having no desire for sexual enjoyment, may be let off after receiving an adequate amount of ransom. p.331

P028 * Those women who have been rescued from the hands of thieves, from floods, in famine, or in national calamities, or who, having been abandoned, missed, or thrown out as if dead in forests, have been taken home may be enjoyed by the rescuer as agreed upon. p.331

P029 * But no such women as have been cast out under royal edict, or by their own kinsmen; nor such as belong to high caste, or do not like to be rescued, nor even those who have children shall be rescued either for ransom or for their person. p.331

P030 [Thus ends Chapter XII, “Sexual Intercourse with Immature Girls,” in Book IV, “The Removal of Thorns” of the Arthasástra of Kautilya. End of the eighty-ninth chapter from the beginning.] p.331

[Thus ends Chapter XII, “Sexual Intercourse with Immature Girls,” in Book IV, “The Removal of Thorns” of the Arthasástra of Kautilya. End of the eighty-ninth chapter from the beginning.]
